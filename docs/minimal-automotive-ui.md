# Minimal automotive UI

Design direction for the 1920x720 car touchscreen:

- designed from scratch rather than restyling the previous dashboard
- large touch targets
- minimal visual noise
- dark graphite background with one restrained cyan accent
- player is the primary screen
- station, track and artist are the dominant information
- playback controls are large and easy to hit
- navigation remains simple and persistent
- no decorative widgets, gradients or dense card grids
- preserve existing playback, metadata, catalog, favorites and steering-wheel behavior
